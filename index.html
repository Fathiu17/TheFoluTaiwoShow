About Me

Hello, I'm Folu Taiwo

FT Your professional photo here

From a classroom in Greensprings, Lagos... to a research desk at WNET New York... and then to the tech laboratory in Lagos, Nigeria — my journey has always been about one thing: using words, wisdom, and technology to make life softer and better.

I am a Tech and Management Consultant, an AI Tutor & Practitioner, and Lead Consultant at Metric Ideas Consulting. For over two decades, I've helped over 20 schools and businesses grow, led digital transformation projects, and convened platforms like the Oyo Educators Summit and Tech Boot Camp for Youth — because I believe young people and women deserve a seat in the digital future.

But behind the consulting, I am at heart a Teacher, Writer, and Speaker. I hold a B.A. in English from the University of Lagos, I'm a Certified Multi-Intelligence Pedagogy Trainer from California, a Cambridge Certified Examiner, and a Certified Project Manager from the UK.

Why The Folu Taiwo Show & Podcast?

After 20 years of training teachers, managing projects, and mentoring women in tech, I wanted a soft space. A space where we don't shout — we talk. A space where we look at the issues of life — Japa, motherhood, business, tiredness, home, hope — with soft eyes. That's why I created The Folu Taiwo Show, and also why I started Ibadan City Mall (ICM) — From Ibadan To The World — caring for Nigerians in the Diaspora.

🎵 Music

Music is my first love. I sing to think, to pray, to heal.

✍️ Writing

Writing is my therapy — education, women, and soft lessons from tech.

🎤 Speaking

From 500 educators at a summit to one market woman — every story deserves a listening ear.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, with studios in Ogun State and Oyo State — but my heart is everywhere Nigerians are: Lagos, London, Toronto, Houston. Welcome to my world!
